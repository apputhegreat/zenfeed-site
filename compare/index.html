Zenfeed vs LinkOff

LinkOff is a free, LinkedIn-only feed customizer with deep content-type filtering; Zenfeed also covers X and YouTube, and adds a timed Rest from AI window LinkOff doesn't have.

Zenfeed vs Control Panel for Twitter

Control Panel for Twitter has 300,000 users and a huge X-only feature set, but doesn't detect AI content or cover LinkedIn/YouTube like Zenfeed does.

Zenfeed vs SocialFocus

SocialFocus is the closest match to Zenfeed's one-extension, multi-platform approach, covering five sites, but it skips LinkedIn and has no AI detection or keyword blocking.

Zenfeed vs AI Content Shield

AI Content Shield blocks AI content across far more sites — Reddit, Facebook, Gmail, and more — while Zenfeed stays free, adds promoted/suggested filtering, and uses a timed auto-restore instead of always-on blocking.

Read the comparison →

Zenfeed vs Clarity

Clarity's community blocklist improves AI detection on YouTube over time through crowdsourcing, but it only covers YouTube and has a small install base compared to Zenfeed's broader platform reach.

Read the comparison →

Zenfeed vs CleanedIn

CleanedIn sends each post to a language model in the cloud to judge it, which catches tone that keyword matching misses. Zenfeed does the matching in your browser, so nothing you read leaves the device — and it stays free and uncapped.
