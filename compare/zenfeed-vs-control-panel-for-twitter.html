Zenfeed vs Control Panel for Twitter

Control Panel for Twitter is a free, open-source X/Twitter customizer with a huge install base and a deep set of timeline and UI controls. Zenfeed covers LinkedIn and X from one install, adds YouTube with a single opt-in toggle, and adds AI-content detection through its Rest from AI timer. Here's how they actually compare.

Why someone might choose Zenfeed

If you're on LinkedIn or YouTube as well as X, Zenfeed covers all three from one install (YouTube after a one-click opt-in) instead of needing a separate X-only tool. It also detects AI-generated content directly through Rest from AI and supports custom keyword blocking, neither of which Control Panel for Twitter offers — its Grok toggle only hides the AI chat button, it doesn't look at post content at all.

Why someone might still want Control Panel for Twitter

Control Panel for Twitter has a much larger install base (300,000 users, 4.7★ across 905 ratings) and a far deeper feature set for X specifically: it defaults to the chronological "Following" timeline and stops X from flipping back to "For You," hides the "For You" tab entirely, hides sidebar and Explore contents, hides or separates Retweets and Quote Tweets, hides engagement metrics and view counts, and offers a full-width timeline plus extensive UI, font, and density customization. It's also open source and runs on Firefox, Edge, and Safari, not just Chrome.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Control Panel for Twitter work on LinkedIn too?

No. Control Panel for Twitter only works on X/Twitter — it doesn't support LinkedIn or YouTube. Zenfeed covers all three from a single install, with YouTube switched on by you in the popup, though it does less there than on the other two. If you only use X and want deep timeline customization, Control Panel for Twitter's broader feature set for that one platform may still win.

Does Control Panel for Twitter detect AI-generated posts?

No. Its Grok toggle only hides the AI chatbot button in X's interface — it doesn't look at post content at all. Zenfeed's Rest from AI sweeps post text for AI-related keywords and phrases on a timer, temporarily hiding matches so real posts surface again. It reads text, not images, so it isn't a perfect detector — but it's the only AI-content feature either tool offers.
